Editorial card for a show in grids and features. `feature` is the one orange block per page (e.g. "On this day").

```jsx
<ShowCard variant="feature" eyebrow="This week in 2014" date="2014-11-01" title="Rosanne Returns" description="…" />
<ShowCard date="2015-03-21" title="The Soul Of Designer Records" trackCount={17} artists={['The Melody Kings','Elizabeth King']} />
```
